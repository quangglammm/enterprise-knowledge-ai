# Customer Returns Process

## Eligibility

Customers can return unwanted goods within 30 days of delivery, provided the goods are unused and in their original packaging. Temperature-controlled cargo and custom-made goods cannot be returned as unwanted goods.

## Arranging a return

The customer requests a return through the customer portal. Bamboo Harbor collects the goods within 3 working days in Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City, and within 5 working days elsewhere.

## Inspection and refund

Returned goods are inspected at the receiving warehouse within 2 working days of arrival. The refund is issued within 5 working days after the inspection is passed.

## Damaged goods

Damaged goods are not handled as returns. Customers follow the claims procedure in the Delivery Service Levels document.
