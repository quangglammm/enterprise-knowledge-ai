# Laptop and Device Policy

## Issue and refresh

Every office employee receives a company-managed laptop. Laptops are refreshed every 3 years, or earlier if repair costs exceed half the price of a new device.

## Security requirements

Full-disk encryption must remain enabled on every laptop. Installing personal software requires approval from the IT service desk.

## Lost or stolen devices

A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT service desk within 24 hours so that it can be locked and wiped remotely.
