# Password and Authentication Standard

## Scope

This standard applies to every account used to access Bamboo Harbor systems, including email, VPN and the HR system.

## Password requirements

Passwords must be at least 14 characters long. Passphrases made of several unrelated words are encouraged. Employees must store work passwords only in the company-approved password manager.

## Rotation

Passwords are not rotated on a fixed schedule. A password must be changed immediately if it may have been exposed or if the IT service desk requests it.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for email, VPN and the HR system. After 10 failed sign-in attempts, an account is locked for 15 minutes.
