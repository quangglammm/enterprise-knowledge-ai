# Delivery Service Levels

## Standard delivery

Standard delivery between Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City is completed within 48 hours. Orders placed before 11:00 for delivery within the same city are delivered on the same day.

## Express delivery

Express delivery between Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City is completed within 24 hours.

## Cold chain

Temperature-controlled cargo is kept between 2 and 8 degrees Celsius from pickup to delivery.

## Claims

Customers must submit claims for damaged goods within 7 days of delivery.
