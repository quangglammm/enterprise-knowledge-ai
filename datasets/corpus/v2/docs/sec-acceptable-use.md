# Acceptable Use Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how employees may use Bamboo Harbor computers, accounts, networks and software. It applies to all employees, contractors and interns.

## Personal use

Limited personal use of company devices is allowed if it does not interfere with work, does not break the law and does not consume significant network capacity. Company accounts must not be used to register for personal online services.

## Generative AI tools

Employees may use only the company-approved AI assistant for work tasks. Confidential and Restricted data must never be pasted into public AI chatbots or translation websites. Output from an AI tool must be checked by the employee before it is sent to a customer.

## Networks

When working outside the office on public or hotel Wi-Fi, employees must connect through the company VPN before opening internal systems.

## Monitoring

Company devices and accounts may be monitored for security purposes in line with the law. Monitoring data is accessible only to the security team.

## Violations

Violations of this policy are handled under the company's disciplinary procedure.
