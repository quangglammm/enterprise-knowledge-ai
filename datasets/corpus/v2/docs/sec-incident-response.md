# Security Incident Response Procedure

Restricted to the security team.

## Severity levels

SEV1: confirmed breach of customer or employee data, or a full outage of the shipment tracking system. SEV2: a contained compromise of a single system. SEV3: a suspicious event that needs investigation.

## Notification

For a SEV1 incident, the incident commander notifies the Chief Information Security Officer within 1 hour of confirmation. A breach of personal data must be reported to the competent authority within 72 hours.

## After the incident

A blameless postmortem is written within 5 working days after every SEV1 or SEV2 incident and shared with engineering leadership.
