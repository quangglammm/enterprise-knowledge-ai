# Delivery Service Levels - Da Nang Hub

These service levels apply to shipments that start or end at the Da Nang hub.

## Standard delivery

Standard delivery between Da Nang and Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City is completed within 36 hours. Orders placed before 10:00 for delivery within Da Nang are delivered on the same day.

## Express delivery

Express delivery between Da Nang and Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City is completed within 18 hours.

## Central provinces

Standard delivery between Da Nang and Hue, Quang Nam or Quang Ngai is completed within 24 hours.
