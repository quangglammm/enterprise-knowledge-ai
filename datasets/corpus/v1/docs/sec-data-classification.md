# Data Classification Policy

## Classification levels

Bamboo Harbor classifies information into four levels: Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted. Every document must show its classification in the header.

## Restricted data

Restricted data includes salary information, national ID numbers and customer contract prices. Restricted data must be encrypted at rest and shared only through the approved file-sharing service. It must never be copied to USB drives.

## Confidential data

Confidential data includes internal financial reports and supplier agreements. It may be shared with employees who need it for their work, but not outside the company without a signed non-disclosure agreement.
